# Aurora Demo Assurance - Gold Plan Policy (Version 2024.1)

This is a FICTIONAL policy written for the ClaimWise AI demo. It is not real insurance terms. Effective 1 January 2024.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Outpatient care means medical treatment that does not require an overnight stay in a hospital, including consultations with a registered physician, minor procedures and diagnostic tests ordered by a physician.

1.2 Inpatient care means treatment that requires admission to a hospital and at least one overnight stay.

1.3 A covered person is a member whose premiums are paid and whose membership is active on the date of service.

## 2. Outpatient benefits

2.1 Annual outpatient limit. The plan pays outpatient expenses up to 5,000 per member per policy year. Expenses above this annual limit are the responsibility of the member.

2.2 Outpatient co-payment. The member pays a co-payment of 20% of each eligible outpatient claim. The plan pays the remaining 80%.

2.3 Consultations and diagnostics. General consultations (procedure code PRC-101 and PRC-102) and ultrasound or laboratory diagnostics (procedure code PRC-140) are covered when ordered by a registered physician and supported by a medical report.

2.4 Physiotherapy is covered for up to 12 sessions per policy year when prescribed by a physician.

## 3. Inpatient benefits

3.1 Hospital admission. Inpatient treatment is covered up to 150,000 per member per policy year, including room, nursing, surgeon fees and operating theatre charges.

3.2 Pre-authorization. Planned admissions require pre-authorization from the insurer at least 3 business days before admission. Emergency admissions must be notified within 48 hours. Claims for planned admissions without pre-authorization may be reduced or declined.

3.3 Inpatient co-payment. The member pays a co-payment of 10% of eligible inpatient charges.

3.4 Surgical procedures. Surgical procedures such as appendectomy (procedure code PRC-310) and cardiac intervention (procedure code PRC-455) are covered when medically necessary and supported by a discharge summary and a medical report.

3.5 Room entitlement. Hospital room charges are covered up to the cost of a semi-private room. The member pays any difference for a private room.

## 4. Pharmacy benefits

4.1 Prescribed medication. Medication is covered when prescribed by a registered physician and dispensed by a licensed pharmacy. A copy of the prescription must accompany the claim.

4.2 Pharmacy co-payment. The member pays 15% of the cost of each covered prescription. Generic substitutes are used where a clinically equivalent generic exists.

4.3 Over-the-counter products, vitamins and nutritional supplements are not covered unless prescribed for a diagnosed deficiency.

## 5. Dental benefits

5.1 Annual dental limit. Dental treatment is covered up to 1,500 per member per policy year.

5.2 Waiting period. A waiting period of 90 days from the membership start date applies to dental benefits, except for dental treatment required after an accident.

5.3 Covered dental treatment. Examinations, cleaning, fillings (procedure code PRC-220) and extractions are covered. Treatment must be supported by a dentist's invoice.

5.4 Orthodontic treatment, including braces and aligners, is excluded.

## 6. General exclusions

6.1 Cosmetic and aesthetic procedures. Procedures performed mainly to improve appearance are excluded, unless needed to repair damage from an accident or a covered surgery.

6.2 Experimental treatment. Treatment that is experimental, unproven or not approved by the relevant health authority is excluded.

6.3 Pre-existing conditions. Conditions diagnosed before the membership start date are excluded for the first 12 months of membership.

6.4 Self-inflicted injury and injury sustained while committing a criminal offence are excluded.

## 7. Claim documentation

7.1 Every claim must include a completed claim form and an itemised invoice.

7.2 Outpatient claims must include a medical report. Inpatient claims must also include a discharge summary. Pharmacy claims must include the prescription.

7.3 Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service. Late claims may be declined.

## 8. Procedure code schedule

8.1 PRC-101 General consultation. PRC-102 Follow-up consultation. PRC-140 Diagnostic ultrasound. PRC-220 Dental filling. PRC-310 Appendectomy. PRC-455 Cardiac intervention.
