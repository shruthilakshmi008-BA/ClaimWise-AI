# Aurora Demo Assurance - Gold Plan Amendment (Version 2025.1)

This is a FICTIONAL amendment written for the ClaimWise AI demo. Effective 1 January 2025. It amends the Gold Plan Policy Version 2024.1.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This amendment replaces selected benefit values in the Gold Plan Policy. Provisions not mentioned here are unchanged.

## 2. Revised outpatient benefits

2.1 Annual outpatient limit. The plan pays outpatient expenses up to 6,000 per member per policy year.

2.2 Outpatient co-payment. The member pays a co-payment of 15% of each eligible outpatient claim.

## 3. Revised claim submission period

3.1 Claims must be submitted within 120 days of the date of service. Late claims may be declined.

## 4. Telemedicine

4.1 Video consultations with a registered physician are covered as outpatient care under the same annual outpatient limit and co-payment as in-person consultations.
