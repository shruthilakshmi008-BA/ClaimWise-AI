# Aurora Demo Assurance - Basic Plan Policy (Version 2024.1)

This is a FICTIONAL policy written for the ClaimWise AI demo. It is not real insurance terms. Effective 1 January 2024.

## 1. Scope of the Basic Plan

1.1 The Basic Plan provides limited cover for outpatient care, inpatient care in emergencies and prescribed medication.

## 2. Outpatient benefits

2.1 Annual outpatient limit. The plan pays outpatient expenses up to 2,000 per member per policy year.

2.2 Outpatient co-payment. The member pays a co-payment of 30% of each eligible outpatient claim.

## 3. Inpatient benefits

3.1 Inpatient treatment is covered only for emergency admissions, up to 40,000 per member per policy year.

3.2 Planned admissions and elective surgery are not covered under the Basic Plan.

## 4. Pharmacy benefits

4.1 Prescribed medication is covered when dispensed by a licensed pharmacy against a prescription. The member pays 25% of each prescription.

## 5. Dental benefits

5.1 Dental treatment is not covered under the Basic Plan, except emergency dental treatment after an accident.

## 6. General exclusions

6.1 Cosmetic procedures, experimental treatment and orthodontic treatment are excluded.

6.2 Pre-existing conditions are excluded for the first 24 months of membership.

## 7. Claim documentation

7.1 Every claim must include a completed claim form and an itemised invoice. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the date of service.
