# Aurora Demo Assurance - Claims Handling Guidelines (Version 3)

This is a FICTIONAL internal guideline written for the ClaimWise AI demo.

## 1. Reviewer responsibilities

1.1 A claims reviewer, not software, makes every approval, rejection or request for information. Automated summaries are an aid only and must be checked against the cited policy wording.

1.2 When the supporting policy wording is missing, unclear or contradictory, the reviewer must record that in the review note and escalate to a supervisor instead of guessing.

## 2. Completeness checks

2.1 Before any decision the reviewer confirms that all required documents for the claim type are present and that the diagnosis and procedure codes, where required, are filled in.

2.2 If documents are missing the claim is moved to Needs Information and the missing items are listed in the note.

## 3. Service levels

3.1 A reviewer should reach a decision within 15 business days of receiving a complete claim.

3.2 Claims with a claimed amount above 50,000 require a second reviewer to confirm the decision.

## 4. Suspicious patterns

4.1 Duplicate invoices, identical claims from the same provider on the same date and amounts just below an approval threshold should be referred to the fraud review team.
